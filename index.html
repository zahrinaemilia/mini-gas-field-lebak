Monte Carlo (3,000 trials)

Inputs are drawn from triangular distributions (lower bound, most likely, upper bound) for: yield, parasitic energy, methane leakage, CAPEX multiplier, digestate sold, service fee, price discount, distance, scrubber cost, pressure reduction station cost, operating days, digestate price.

Calculation boundary: CO2 from burning Bio CNG is not counted (biogenic carbon). N2O from digestate and the fertilizer it displaces are excluded. Results depend on landfill conditions (MCF) that have not been measured in Lebak.
